## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1. Flip, do not split.** The card offered two remedies: flip P.1
  to `partial`, or split the release clause into its own row. Flipping
  keeps the charter's row set unchanged (Part III rows are the
  charter's, not the implementation's to add to) and matches the
  precedent III.C row 4 sets for a met core with a named remainder.
- **D2. The residual has one owner.** The note names the unmet clause
  and the step that closes it: promotion section 5 step 2 cuts the
  first `seed/v*` release. No new card, no new phase item.
- **D3. One statement of what remains.** The Phase 12 exit record in
  `next/docs/progress.md` and the promotion packet in
  `next/docs/promotion.md` are the sources the conformance table
  transcribes, so they change with it (review finding on #340). The
  generated report is regenerated, never hand-edited.
