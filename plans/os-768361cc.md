# Plan: next promotion milestone — spin-out criteria and the critical path (os-768361cc)

Amends [`docs/next-build-plan.md`](../docs/next-build-plan.md) with the
milestone it names twice and never defines. The ground rules say
`scripts/seed` (v1) "remains the only coordination entry point for
doing the work **until spin-out**"; the autonomy contract lists
"publishing/spin-out" among the few escalations. Nothing else in the
plan says what spin-out **is**, what must be true before it, what the
cutover looks like, or how to fall back. Thirteen phases of
conformance work therefore have no stated finish line for the thing
the work is for: Seed taking over. This amendment supplies it.

The precedent is #157: a routing binds only when the phasing
authority's own text says it. So this task changes the build plan
itself, not the frontier's prose about it.

## The frame this amendment encodes

Seed's spine is already coherent: one append-only signed history;
legality decided by tables at an admission boundary; derived,
position-stamped, byte-identical projections; and — since Phase 8 —
affordances computed from the *same* rule set admission enforces,
with drift a named bug class. Every claim about the world is derived
from one history, stamped with the position it was derived at, and
accompanied by the rules that produced it.

An agent operating that system runs a five-step loop: **orient**
(what is true, what is mine, what changed), **choose** (which legal
act to take), **act**, **verify**, **learn**. Seed today is strong at
*act* (admission decides legality, not convention) and at *verify*
(positions, receipts, verdicts, projections). It is thin at *orient*
and *choose*, and it has no delivery moment for *learn*.

The gap has one root cause, and naming it is what makes the
remainder small: **Seed represents permission but not obligation.**
Affordances answer "what may I do"; nothing answers "what is owed, by
whom, since when, and what discharges it" — although every fact
needed to derive that is already in the ledger (a claim with a lease,
a submission awaiting a verdict, an unsettled run, a blocked contract
awaiting a human). Obligation is a *projection over the fold*, not a
new authority: same idiom, same guarantees, no new source of truth.

That is why loop-completeness is a promotion criterion and not a
polish item: a lane that cannot orient or choose cannot run
unattended, whatever the conformance report says.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — promotion is two steps, and only the first is ours to
  decide.** *Self-hosting*: this repository's own development
  coordinates on Seed, with v1 retained read-only for history.
  *Distribution*: Seed becomes what new users clone. The autonomy
  contract already routes publishing/spin-out to a human, so this
  amendment defines the criteria and the mechanics of **self-hosting**
  in full, and for distribution defines only the readiness gate,
  leaving the act itself an escalation.
- **D2 — the criteria are stated as checkable conditions, not
  phases.** Promotion is met when, at the enforced self-hosted
  posture: (1) **loop-completeness** — a lane can run poll → claim →
  plan-gate → work → meter → submit → verdict → merge-observe →
  deliberate exit, plus escalation and messages, entirely through
  Seed verbs, orienting from one position-stamped read rather than
  hand-assembling ledger payloads and hand-computing fences;
  (2) **lanes operable** — Phase 9 complete (role fragments,
  dispatcher least-capability, injection corpus green, worker loop
  with exhaustion parking, escalation with packet/question/decision,
  maintenance runnable unattended); (3) **migration proven** — Phase
  12's `seed import --from-open-seed` drilled against a real export
  of *this* repository's v1 state, not only a fixture;
  (4) **shadow run** — Seed coordinates a declared slice of this
  repository's own cards beside v1 for a stated window, with any
  divergence reconciled and recorded; (5) **cutover and rollback
  written down** — which entry point flips when, what stays
  authoritative where during the window, and the documented path
  back; (6) **core conformance** — every pillar's mechanisms at the
  enforced self-hosted posture with `doctor` reporting exactly which
  Phase 13 rows remain open.
- **D3 — Phases 10, 11, and 13 are explicitly NOT promotion
  blockers.** The plan already frames Phases 0–12 as core conformance
  with Phase 13 as deliberately deferred completion; promoting at
  core conformance is therefore consistent with the plan's own
  structure rather than a weakening of it. Evaluation infrastructure
  (10), curator and flywheel (11), and Phase 13's deferred rows
  continue **after** promotion, on a system that is by then
  coordinating its own development — which is strictly better
  feedback than continuing to build them against a fixture. The
  amendment says this in the plan's own words so no later reader
  treats promotion as requiring all thirteen phases.
- **D4 — the amendment adds one build-plan item, because
  loop-completeness has no home today.** Phase 9 defines the lanes
  but nothing defines the surface those lanes act through: the CLI
  today is the raw protocol seam (`ledger append --verb claim.taken
  --payload …` with fences read out of a projection by hand and
  four-part packets hand-built). Add **Phase 9 item 5 — the
  lane-facing surface**: (a) an **obligations projection** deriving,
  per subject and actor, what is owed, since which position, under
  which clock, and which verbs discharge it — a projection like every
  other (deterministic, byte-identical, position-stamped,
  non-authoritative), never a new authority; (b) a **situation read**
  answering "what is true for me now", with `--since <position>`
  returning only what changed, so a resuming lane pays for the delta
  rather than reconstructing the world; (c) **loop verbs** that
  derive every argument the system can derive (fences from the active
  window, base ranges from the repository, reservation ids from the
  budget view) and refuse — before signing — anything the tables
  would refuse after, naming the act that would succeed. This is the
  same principle Phase 8 landed one level down: enforcement and
  advertisement from one rule set, now applied to *argument
  construction* and *situation*, so the CLI makes the legal act cheap
  and the illegal act hard to express. Its own card and plan
  implement it; this amendment only names the obligation, per #157.
- **D5 — the critical path is stated, with what parallelizes.**
  Phase 9 (including the new item 5) → the Phase 12 subset promotion
  needs (preseed init, `import --from-open-seed`, operator handbook,
  credential-free simulation) → shadow run → cutover. Phase 10 and
  Phase 11 may proceed in parallel after Phase 9 but gate nothing;
  Phase 12's compromised-actor drill remains the release gate for
  *distribution* and is therefore on the second step's path, not the
  first. Phase 13 follows promotion entirely.
- **D6 — scope guard.** This amendment defines and routes; it
  implements nothing. No phase renumbering, no dependency changes, no
  new conformance criteria beyond the promotion section's own
  checklist (which cites charter rows rather than inventing them).
  The promotion section is explicitly **not** a Part III criterion:
  Part III is the charter's, and promotion is the plan's.

## Steps

1. **Add the promotion section** to `docs/next-build-plan.md` after
   the phase list (a new top-level section beside the backlog), in
   the plan's own register: the two-step definition per D1, the six
   checkable criteria per D2, the not-required list per D3, and the
   critical path per D5.
2. **Add Phase 9 item 5** per D4, in Phase 9's own words, with the
   obligations projection, the situation read (including `--since`),
   and the loop verbs named as one deliverable, and a sentence tying
   it to the promotion criterion it satisfies.
3. **Point the phases at the milestone**: one sentence on Phase 9 and
   one on Phase 12 noting that these phases carry promotion gates, so
   a reader arriving at a phase sees the finish line without reading
   the whole plan.
4. **Record the decision** in `next/docs/decisions.md`: promotion
   defined, promoting at core conformance rather than full Part III,
   Phases 10/11/13 explicitly post-promotion, and the reasoning that
   a self-hosting system produces better feedback for those phases
   than a fixture does.
5. `make check`; receipt; evidence; review.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The plan carries a promotion section defining self-hosting and
   distribution, with distribution's act left as the existing
   escalation.
2. The six criteria are stated as conditions a reader can check, each
   naming where it is satisfied (phase item, drill, or written
   artifact) rather than as prose aspiration.
3. Phases 10, 11, and 13 are named in the plan as **not** promotion
   blockers, with the core-conformance reasoning stated.
4. Phase 9 carries item 5 (obligations projection, situation read
   with `--since`, loop verbs) in its own words, tied to the
   loop-completeness criterion.
5. Phase 9 and Phase 12 each carry a pointer sentence to the
   milestone.
6. `next/docs/decisions.md` records the promotion decision and its
   reasoning.
7. `make check` green (docs-only).
