# os-74ce2261 — the cache carries the event's ts, so evidence is queryable by time

Charter III.G row 10 ("Evidence, receipts, and verdicts are queryable
by contract, actor, time, and outcome"), recorded UNMET at the Phase
10 exit: the SQLite cache projection (`next/spec/projections.md`;
`next/internal/project/cache.go`) answers contract, actor and outcome,
and answers time only by position, which is order, not a clock. No
table carries the event's `ts`. This card adds it.

## What the tree actually shows

- The cache's per-event tables — `contracts` (subject, position, verb,
  actor, payload), `actor_history`, `actor_signed`, `offers`,
  `reservations`, `runs`, `interrupts` — key every row by position;
  `contract_state` carries the derived verdict columns. The event
  envelope's `ts` (RFC 3339 UTC, "recorded for humans, never an
  ordering authority") is dropped at projection time.
- The cache is a registered projection: byte-identical rebuilds from
  any verified chain, a version (`cacheVersion`) that bumps whenever
  the derivation changes so an already-published tree republishes
  under the new build id, and the stamp table naming the position,
  tip and version it was built at.
- `projections.md` says what the cache is and drills its indexed
  lookups; the III.D row is met and this card changes nothing about
  it.

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

**D1. `ts` is a column on every per-event table, filled from the
envelope.** `contracts`, `actor_history`, `actor_signed`, `offers`,
`reservations`, `runs` and `interrupts` gain `ts TEXT NOT NULL`, the
event's `ts` string verbatim (RFC 3339 UTC sorts lexically, so a range
is a string comparison and no parsing is stored), placed after
`position`. Derived tables (`contract_state`, `queue`, `report`) gain
nothing: they are not events. Refused: a parsed or normalized instant
(the envelope's string is the record); a `ts` on a derived row.

**D2. The next cache generation, under the stamp discipline.**
`cacheVersion` bumps by one from whatever the tree carries when this
card lands (item 4 of Phase 12 takes 13; this card takes the next),
the build id and the stamp follow, and the drills that pin the
generation move with it. Refused: adding the column without the bump
(an already-published cache would keep its build id and lack the
column).

**D3. Queryable by time, drilled.** A drill builds the cache from a
fixture chain whose events carry distinct instants, and queries
`contracts` by a `ts` range (`WHERE ts >= ? AND ts < ?`) and by
contract, actor, time and outcome together (the row's four names in
one query), asserting the rows the chain says. The spec's cache
paragraph gains the sentence; the Phase 10 exit's routing of III.G
row 10 is answered in `next/docs/progress.md`.

**D4. Bounds.** No new verb, no transition row, no protocol version;
`ts` stays what the charter says it is (never an ordering authority:
position orders, `ts` is what a human asked for). The other
projections are untouched.
