## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — the record follows the Phase 12 shape exactly.** One bold
  opening naming the exit line's criteria and the drills that back
  them; the pillars walked row by row with "met by" citations; the
  routed rows resolved or re-routed in those words; the closing
  sentence naming this card as the record's task PR.
- **D2 — the table is flipped by the record and nothing else.** Every
  Phase 13 row in `next/spec/conformance.json` moves from `open` to
  the status the record states, with the record as evidence, and
  `conformance.md` is regenerated; a row the record leaves `partial`
  or `routed` carries the routing in its note. No row is flipped
  without a sentence in the record that says why.
- **D3 — complete means every row met.** The charter admits a
  conformance claim only when every Part III criterion holds, so the
  record quotes the doctor's `conformance` section on the fixture
  deployment at the enforced posture and closes the phase only when
  it reports `complete: true`, which means no row `open`, `partial` or
  `routed`, III.R included. While any row is outstanding the record
  says UNMET for it, names where its evidence will come from, and the
  phase does not close: the record is written when the doctor says
  complete, and the card waits until then. This survived an amendment
  that tried to drop the wait (#315, review by
  chatgpt-codex-connector). Two reasons it stands. The build plan's
  critical path is explicit — "Phase 12 in full → shadow run → the
  escalated self-hosting cutover → the escalated distribution step.
  Phase 13 follows" — and Phase 13's own exit line requires the
  conformance report to show Part III complete, so writing the record
  first authorizes this card out of order. And the deadlock the
  amendment claimed is not one: D2's flips are applied on the
  implementation branch, and the doctor runs against that checkout, so
  `complete: true` is reachable there once III.R's evidence is on
  `main` without this record having merged.

- **D4 — the frontier follows the build plan's critical path.** That
  path (build plan §5) is Phase 12 in full, then the shadow run, then
  the escalated self-hosting cutover, then the escalated distribution
  step, and Phase 13's exit after them, since III.R's rows are met by
  promotion's measurements. The Frontier states exactly that: Phase
  13's items land as they do, the promotion evidence is presented and
  stopped at (neither cutover is autonomously decidable), and this
  record closes the phase only once the promotion steps and their
  evidence exist. It never re-derives promotion as something that
  follows Phase 13's close.
- **D5 — one learning sentence.** What the phase learned, pointing at
  `memory/LEARNINGS.md`.
