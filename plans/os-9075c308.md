# Plan: next — Phase 11 item 5, flywheel v0: recurring-shape detection from the ledger, a drafted deterministic workflow, mock validation through the v1 engine, the proposal PR; conversion rate tracked (os-9075c308)

The build plan's Phase 11 item 5: *"Flywheel v0: recurring-shape
detection from the ledger → drafted workflow → mock validation →
proposal PR."* The charter (§12): *"Recurring trajectory shapes are
detected from the ledger, drafted as deterministic workflows,
validated in mock, proposed as PRs; … the registry is protected,
silent self-modification impossible. Conversion rate (recurring
chores → merged workflows) is a tracked metric. Every chore an agent
does twice becomes infrastructure — through gates."* III.K row 8 is
the target, and the Phase 11 exit line names the proof: *"a real
recurring chore in the fixture converts to a workflow through the
gates."*

This plan is written against item 1's `curate` grant (os-f30ee0d3,
#226) and depends on it merging first; it touches nothing items 2
through 4 touch.

## What the tree actually shows

Measured, not assumed:

- **The workflow engine exists, and it is v1's.** `.seed/workflows/
  *.yaml` (`schema_version: "1"`, `name`, `steps` with `run` or
  `role` + `prompt`, `depends_on`, `consumes` / `produces`,
  `output_format`; `defaults`, `budgets`) is validated by
  `scripts/seed workflow validate <file>|--all`, which `make check`
  runs through `scripts/validate.sh`, and mock-run by `scripts/seed
  workflow run <name> --mock` with zero credentials, commands recorded
  and never executed, gates auto-passed. `scripts/seed` bootstraps the
  pinned engine (download, SHA-256 against `.seed/engine.lock`, a
  cache under `$HOME/.cache`) and honors `SEED_ENGINE=<path>`.
  `.seed/**` is a protected path: PR plus owner review, write-denied
  to agent keys. §13's engine is therefore not something `next/`
  lacks; it is the integration point this card names.
- **Nothing in `next/` knows a workflow**, and the protocol catalog
  names no `workflow.*` verb.
- **A contract's shape is on the record.** `intent.filed` carries
  `routing`, `tier`, `budget` and the intent text; `contract.specified`
  the acceptance ref (`<path> @ <commit>`, `executable`, `gate`); the
  lifecycle and the facts on the subject (offer, run, verdict, merge)
  are admitted verbs in order; `done` is the terminal. Nothing groups
  subjects by shape or counts recurrence.
- **The report projection is the metrics surface** (version "11"
  after Phase 10 item 5's `lanes` section), record-derivable and
  repository-free.
- **The curator proposes and gates dispose** (item 1): a `curate` key
  proposes hypotheses through `hypothesis.proposed`; the manifest's
  posture is "proposes everything, approves nothing".
- **Gate-before-run holds for acceptance content**: a tool that reads
  a spec's commands reads gated content only (`acceptance.md`).

## Design decisions (binding for this task)

- **D1 — a shape is record-derivable, and recurrence is counted, not
  judged.** `internal/flywheel` (new). For every `done` subject the
  shape is the JCS form of `{routing, acceptance_path, tier,
  sequence}`, `sequence` the subject's admitted verbs in order with
  positions, actors, payloads and instants dropped; the id is
  `s-<12 hex>` of that form. A shape is **recurring** when at least
  `flywheel.RecurringAfter` done subjects fold to it, a constant the
  spec states (`3`: "every chore an agent does twice" is the charter's
  figure, and the third occurrence is the record's proof that the
  second was not the last), mirrored under a spec pin like the tier
  table's. `seed flywheel shapes --ledger|--remote` lists shapes with
  their counts and occurrences.

  Refused: detection from intent prose (similarity is a model's
  judgment, and the record carries the structural fields the chore
  actually repeats). Refused: detection from Phase 10 item 5's
  trajectories (a lane's decision points; a chore is a contract's
  shape across lanes).

- **D2 — the draft is a v1 workflow, deterministic, derived from the
  shape and the gated acceptance.** `seed flywheel draft --shape
  <id> --ledger <dir> --repo <dir> --out <file>` writes a workflow
  named `chore-<shape prefix>`: `description` from the routing and
  the acceptance path in the schema's "Use when / NOT for" form;
  `inputs` the fields that vary across the occurrences (`goal`, the
  intent text; `anchor`, the acceptance commit); one `run` step per
  validation command of the acceptance spec at its GATED anchor, in
  the spec's order, each command verbatim and each depending on the
  previous; and one role step per judgment point the sequence
  carries, mapped lane to role (`claim` span to `implementer`,
  `verdict.rendered` to `reviewer` with the smoke workflow's JSON
  verdict `output_format`, `plan.proposed` to `planner`), whose
  prompts are templates over the inputs and the produced artifacts
  and nothing else. Deterministic in both senses: every `run` is a
  command the spec already ran, and two drafts of one shape are
  byte-identical. A shape whose acceptance is not gated at every
  occurrence refuses `ungated`: the drafter reads gated content only.

- **D3 — mock validation runs through the v1 engine, the one
  integration point.** `seed flywheel draft --validate` invokes the
  repository's `scripts/seed workflow validate <file>` and
  `scripts/seed workflow run <name> --mock` as subprocesses (honoring
  `SEED_ENGINE`) and refuses the draft when either fails, naming the
  engine's finding verbatim. The v1 surfaces touched: `scripts/seed
  workflow` invoked, `.seed/workflow-schema/workflow.schema.json`
  read by a drill; nothing in v1 is modified. The engine-backed drill
  runs where the engine is (`make check` bootstraps it through
  `validate.sh`; CI's `check` job) and, with neither `SEED_ENGINE`
  nor a bootstrapped cache present, skips BY NAME with the reason in
  its output rather than passing; every next-side derivation (the
  shape, the draft's bytes against a committed golden, the proposal
  rules, the report) is drilled with no engine, the unprivileged run
  included. The skip is named in the plan because it is the one
  place this card's evidence depends on a binary `next/` does not
  build.

- **D4 — the proposal is a PR and a fact, and the registry stays
  protected.** `workflow.proposed` (new namespace `workflow.*`,
  catalog growth; `curate` alone, the proposal posture) carries
  `{"shape": "<s-id>", "workflow": "<path @ commit>", "occurrences":
  ["<contract>@<position>", …], "validated": {"run": "<mock run id>"}}`;
  the boundary requires at least `RecurringAfter` distinct admitted
  `done` occurrences each folding to the named shape (recomputed from
  the record at admission), the path under `.seed/workflows/`, and no
  standing unmerged proposal for the shape. `seed flywheel propose`
  writes the validated draft on branch `seed/flywheel-<shape>`,
  appends the fact citing the file at that commit, and prints the PR
  to open; the PR is the harness's to open, as a plan PR is today.
  `workflow.merged` (`observer`, `operator`, the merge-observation
  row) carries `{"workflow": "<path @ merged-commit>", "shape",
  "pr": "<pr @ merged-commit>"}` and cites an admitted proposal. The
  tool never writes under `.seed/workflows/` on `main`: the file
  reaches the registry only through the PR the governance root
  reviews, which is "silent self-modification impossible" as the tree
  already enforces it for every protected path.

- **D5 — the conversion rate is a report section, version "12".**
  `flywheel: {recurring, proposed, merged, conversion_rate}`, the rate
  merged over recurring as a three-decimal string, null at zero;
  null when no work subject exists. `seed flywheel status` renders
  the same rows from the fold.

- **D6 — the fixture chore converts.** The modes fixture gains a chore
  worked three times: three contracts with one routing and one
  acceptance spec path, each through claim, submission, verdict and
  merge to `done`; `seed flywheel shapes` reports the shape recurring
  at three; `draft --validate` yields a workflow the engine accepts
  and mock-runs green (the engine-backed arm); `propose` appends the
  fact on the branch; the observer records `workflow.merged`; the
  report shows one recurring, one proposed, one merged, `1.000`. That
  is the exit line's proof, in the fixture.

- **D7 — versioning.** Two verbs under a new namespace are catalog
  growth (item 1's D5 posture): no protocol bump, every existing chain
  verifies byte for byte; the report version moves.

- **D8 — scope guard.** No repair role (a failing step's repair role
  proposing a patch is named as a later item; v0 stops at the proposal
  PR, as the build plan's text does); no workflow engine in `next/`;
  no write under `.seed/workflows/` on `main`; no forge call (the PR
  is the harness's); no prose similarity, no model; no change to the
  v1 engine, its schema or `validate.sh`; no Makefile change; no
  change to `transitions.json`, `internal/loop` or `next/lanes` beyond
  the curator fragment naming the flywheel.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Boundary set (new, shown working):**

1. **Shapes.** Two done subjects with one routing, one acceptance path,
   one tier and one verb sequence fold to one id; a different routing,
   path, tier or sequence folds to another; the id is stable across
   positions, actors, payloads and instants; a subject not at `done`
   counts nothing; recurrence holds at `RecurringAfter` and not below,
   and the constant equals the figure `flywheel.md` states.
2. **The draft.** Two drafts of one shape are byte-identical and equal
   the committed golden; the `run` steps are the spec's validation
   commands verbatim, in order, each depending on the previous; the
   role steps map the sequence's judgment points lane to role with
   prompts over inputs and artifacts only; `inputs` are exactly the
   varying fields; a shape with an ungated occurrence refuses
   `ungated`.
3. **Validation.** With the engine present, `scripts/seed workflow
   validate` accepts the draft and `--mock` runs green through `draft
   --validate`; a planted draft with a dangling `depends_on` is refused
   naming the engine's finding verbatim; without the engine the drill
   skips by name with the reason printed, and the golden drill still
   runs.
4. **Proposal and merge.** `workflow.proposed` admits from `curate`
   with `RecurringAfter` distinct admitted done occurrences folding to
   the shape and a path under `.seed/workflows/`; it refuses fewer
   occurrences, a duplicate occurrence, a non-done one, one folding to
   another shape, a path elsewhere, a standing unmerged proposal for
   the shape, and from a `claim` key or a root holding no `curate`;
   `workflow.merged` admits from `observer` citing an admitted proposal
   and refuses citing anything else or from `curate`; `propose` writes
   the file on `seed/flywheel-<shape>` and refuses to write on `main`.
5. **Report.** `flywheel` counts recurring, proposed and merged with
   the rate over recurring; null at no work subject; version "12"
   republishes existing prefixes; `seed flywheel status` agrees.
6. **End to end.** The fixture chore worked three times converts:
   shape at three, draft validated, proposed on the branch, merged by
   observation, `1.000` in the report; the draft file is absent from
   `main`.
7. **Mutation evidence.** Each must fail a drill: the shape ignoring
   routing, path, tier or sequence; recurrence compared with `>`
   instead of `>=`; the draft inventing a command not in the spec; the
   draft reading ungated content; the draft's prompts carrying intent
   text verbatim; the proposal admitting fewer occurrences or one of
   another shape; the path check dropped; the duplicate check dropped;
   `workflow.merged` without a proposal; the rate over proposed
   instead of recurring; the engine subprocess's failure ignored; the
   propose verb writing on `main`.
8. `make check` green with coverage measured **cold**, at least three
   readings above the gate, and the suites pass **unprivileged** under
   `setpriv --reuid=65534 --regid=65534 --clear-groups` (the
   engine-backed arm skipping by name there, everything else green).

**Retention set (existing, shown unharmed):**

- `.seed/workflows/` on `main`, `validate.sh`, the schema and the
