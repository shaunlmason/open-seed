Phase 9 item 1, second of three (1a landed as #188; 1c is planned in
#189). III.J's second row is HALF MET: 1a made the dispatcher's standing
claim checkable as an allowlist over its manifest grants, but the paired
half of that row is input handling against a hostile corpus:

  "The dispatcher runs with least standing capability and passes the
  injection conformance suite (hostile corpus: embedded instructions in
  intents, mirrors, and tool output are quoted as data, never obeyed)."

The honest framing this card has to take: there is no model under next/,
so "never obeyed" is not directly testable here, and a suite that
pretended otherwise would be theatre. The charter itself names the way
out (Part II, Data/instruction defense-in-depth): "a model can still be
persuaded by adversarial text it reads - which is why capability bounds,
not fencing, carry the invariant." So the suite proves that obeying the
text CHANGES NOTHING: the dispatcher's key cannot perform any act
outside dispatch whatever the payload says, hostile text refuses or
survives as an inert quoted string, and nothing interpolates it into a
command position.

Scope: the corpus, the drills, and the spec passage that states what the
suite does and does not establish. No new verb, no new capability, no
change to the admission rules the corpus is fired at.
