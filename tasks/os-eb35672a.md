Two review findings on #194, both verified correct, arriving after that
PR merged. A merged PR cannot track new work, so they land fresh. The
fixes are already written and mutation-checked on the local branch
seed/os-378e44f3 at commit 35bb194.

P1 - the identity check ran once at the top of Step. That leaves the
work step, the longest part of an iteration and the likeliest moment
for a key rotation to land, as a window in which the settle and the
exit then sign as a NEW identity. A window opened by one actor and
closed by another, or left open because the close refused, is exactly
the state the four deliberate exits exist to make impossible. Fix: the
check moves into act(), so it covers every act rather than the first.
Drilled by rotating the key from inside the work callback and asserting
the iteration stops at the next act with no settle, submission or park
reaching the seam.

P2 - and this is the worse one. The packet-unlink drill was ADDED in
response to #193's finding that the behavior was unasserted, and it
asserted the wrong thing under the right name: it ran a successful
writePacket, checked the file existed, called the success-path cleanup,
and passed, while both error-branch unlinks could be deleted with no
effect. Confirmed by deleting them. Fix: inject a failing writer and a
failing closer through a seam added for the purpose, following the
injection precedent internal/transition sets, so each post-creation
branch is genuinely entered.

That was the seventh drill this cycle to agree with a convenient shape
rather than the shipped one, and the only one written in direct
response to a finding about that same class. The check that catches
every member of the family costs one command: delete the behavior,
re-run the drill.

Also carries the spec passage (identity is re-derived before every act,
not once per iteration), the decisions entries, and the learnings note.
Coverage measured cold on the branch: 91.1% against the 90% gate; a
single 88.3% reading was the known counter-loss flake (os-cafba959),
identifiable by the whole package reading implausibly low at once.
