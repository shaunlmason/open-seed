Found while planning os-52d5da3f (review finding on #170). next/internal/admit/admit.go gates ALL budget verbs on s.State == in_progress (the isBudget branch), so once a holder reserves and then leaves the window — submission.made to review, or a park/reap to blocked/ready — the open reservation can no longer be settled or released: both closing verbs refuse with invalid_transition, and the capacity stays consumed in BudgetViewAt's derivation with no admissible act to free it. Phase 7 landed the reserve/close pair inside the window and never decided the outside-window case. Two candidate resolutions, for the plan to choose between on evidence: (a) admission change — let the prior holder or the operator lane close a reservation whose window has ended, which needs the fence rule worked through (the close cites a fence that is no longer active) and a spec amendment in next/spec/budgets.md; or (b) maintenance — treat a reservation whose window ended as a finding for Phase 9 item 3's unattended maintenance loop to reap, exactly as the Phase 7 exit routed unsettled-run detection there, with the reap recorded as an ordinary audited act. Option (b) matches the existing routing precedent and needs no admission change; option (a) is the one that lets a human recover capacity directly. Note for whoever plans this: os-52d5da3f deliberately emits its budget.open obligation ONLY inside the live window, because outside it the advertised dischargers would be unreachable and an obligation nobody can discharge is an anomaly, not an obligation. Plan-first (L2).
