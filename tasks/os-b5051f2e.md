plans/os-16e55c11.md D5 words the guardrail bar as "zero guardrail breaches (no refusal followed by a blind retry; every claim within its ceiling once item 4 lands)". Neither clause is implemented, and neither is reachable from the chain alone as simulate.Audit is written.

The ceiling. admit.Default()'s policy rule "ceiling" refuses a claim.taken whose subject tier is above the squad's agent ceiling, for an actor enrolled as an agent. It reads Context.Declaration, the deployment declaration in seed.json, and internal/admit is explicit that this is admission policy rather than chain validity: a raw-pushed claim above the ceiling folds as filed and the chain still verifies byte for byte. simulate.Audit takes []*event.Record only, so two byte-identical chains are compliant or not depending on a file the ledger does not carry.

What a card would have to decide: whether seed ledger audit loads the repo's declaration and threads it into simulate.Audit (mirroring admission, which is a no-op with no declaration), or whether the bar stays ledger-only and III.R row 5's wording is corrected to say the ceiling is not among the breaches a chain can show. Either way the change reaches next/internal/simulate/audit.go and next/cmd/seed/ledger.go, both outside plans/os-aaec6a3c.md's D5 bounds, which is why it is carded rather than folded in there.

The blind-retry clause. A refused append never lands, so a refusal followed by a retry leaves only the retry in the chain. That clause is not observable from the ledger at all and the same card should say so rather than leave it implied.
