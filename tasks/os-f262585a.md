The tree sits at ~90.1% aggregate coverage over ./internal/... — right on the covergate -gate 90 line. Because 'seed receipt verify --run' does its own separate COLD 'make check' (a fresh coverage collection with a few tenths of run-to-run variance), a receipt generated and green at 90.1% can have its verify dip under 90 on a bad draw and fail as a 'receipt mismatch' — a per-PR flake that costs a regenerate-and-rerun each time. Observed directly on os-ad610334: commit b8b6335's verify failed while 54aac5e (a fresh regen at the same 90.1%) passed; #282/#286/#289 all draw from the same lottery.

Fix: raise the REAL aggregate to ~91% with tests on the thin paths so a bad draw stays >=90. Concrete starting points already measured on internal/protections (forgejo.go/observer.go): the error branches of Apply, putBranch, putTag, do, and Merged (each ~80-85%); plus a survey of the other lowest-covered internal packages for cheap, honest gains. This is the kind of coverage os-ad610334 already began (its coverage_extra_test.go took protections from 89.7% to ~90.2%); this card carries it tree-wide.

Bounds: TESTS ONLY. No change to cmd/covergate's -gate value, no perf/budgets.json ceiling move, no product behavior — only new tests exercising real paths. Note cmd/seed is NOT in -coverpkg=./internal/... so it does not count toward the gate; focus on ./internal/... Tier: trivial in code, plan-first for the target and the package list. Not conformance-blocking, but it ends the rerun lottery for every task PR.
