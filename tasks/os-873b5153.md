The follow-on the interleaving check's plan names (plans/os-07e6e76c.md D7, "the verdict-then-merge reconciliation and racing mode are the named follow-on, filed once this explorer's shape is known, not this card's"). That card merged as #359, so the shape is known and this is filable. Charter sections extended: SEED-NEXT.md §II.8 (done is a verdict AND a reconciliation, across two systems that share no transaction, with divergence a first-class detected state) and §II.6 racing mode (duplicate execution tolerated, first verified success settles). No Part III row changes; not schedulable ahead of an open Phase 13 row.

WHAT EXISTS. os-07e6e76c built an explorer in `next/internal/gitref`: an abstract state machine whose alphabet is the append loop's own steps, walked depth-first over every interleaving for small N with memoization, properties checked at every state, and its terminal traces replayed through real clients so the model is evidence about the tree rather than about itself. Four configurations walk 18 to 1,151 states in under fifty milliseconds; the replay is the cost, not the enumeration. `internal/reconcile` already classifies divergence as a pure function of the lifecycle fold, with the evidence-grade classes named beside the record-derivable ones. Racing mode is live at seed/6: claims admit below the declared cap, each racer carries its own fence, and `internal/maintain`'s pass reaps the settled-out racers.

THE GAP, IN TWO PARTS.

1. RECONCILIATION. `verdict.rendered → merge.requested → merge.observed → done` spans the ledger and the forge, which share no transaction, and the charter calls every arrow its own event with divergence a detected state rather than a silent assumption. What exists is a classifier and tests over hand-built folds. Nothing enumerates the interleavings of a verifier, a merger and a forge that moves on its own: a merge that lands before its observation, an observation of a merge that never happened, a force-push on the target between request and observation, a verdict rendered while a merge is in flight. The property has two halves and both need saying: every class in `internal/reconcile` is REACHABLE by some interleaving (a class nothing can produce is dead code pretending to be a guard), and NOTHING ELSE is reachable (an interleaving that produces an unclassified divergence is a hole in the vocabulary).

2. RACING MODE. The settlement rule is a concurrency property: below the cap two claims coexist, the first VERIFIED success settles, later exits are claim-scoped, and the reaper clears the rest with a packet naming the settlement. `internal/maintain/racing_test.go` drives this through hand-written record sequences. Nothing enumerates the interleavings: two racers submitting at once, a racer exiting between another's submission and its verdict, a reap arriving while a settled-out racer's own exit is in flight, a second racer's verdict landing after the first settles.

WHAT THIS CARD OWES. The explorer generalized to a second and third alphabet, with the same discipline: properties at every state, and terminal traces replayed against the real surfaces so neither model is evidence about itself. The reconciliation model's steps are the four verbs plus a forge that merges, refuses and force-pushes on its own schedule; its properties are the two halves of reachability above, plus "no interleaving reaches `done` without an authentic pass" and "every divergence the model produces is one `internal/reconcile` names". The racing model's steps are claim, submit, verdict, settle, exit and reap under a declared cap; its properties are the cap, exactly one settlement, claim-scoped exits, and the reaper leaving no active claim of a settled race. Replay drives admission (`admit.Check`) for the ledger half, as the sibling drives `Fetch` and `attempt`.

The plan decides: whether the explorer becomes a shared harness (its state machine is generic over a step alphabet; the gitref one would move behind it) or is copied per alphabet, and the cost of each — a shared harness is one instrument to keep honest, a copy is one less coupling between packages that share nothing else; where each model lives (`internal/reconcile` and `internal/maintain` are the obvious homes, and the external-test-package plus export-shim pattern from os-21bf939f applies wherever the audit or a consumer imports the package under test); how the forge is modeled (a process with its own steps, or an oracle the model consults); the sizes and where they split between `make check-next` and the weekly job.

NOT THIS CARD. The append loop and halt (os-07e6e76c, done). A third instrument of a different kind: this is the same explorer pointed at two more state spaces, and if the plan finds the two halves want different harnesses, it says so and this card splits.
